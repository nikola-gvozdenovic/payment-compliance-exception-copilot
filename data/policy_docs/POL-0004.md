# AML / Sanctions Screening Policy

## Rule

Every payment's debtor and creditor names are screened, at ingestion, against the sanctions
and watchlist entries maintained in `data/sanctions_mock/`. Screening is performed on both
exact and approximate (fuzzy) name matches, since sanctioned entities frequently operate under
name variants or transliterations.

## Enforcement

A name match — exact or approximate — against any entry in `data/sanctions_mock/` results in
the payment being flagged with cause `compliance_hold` and held in `flagged` status pending
review by the Compliance Agent. The processing log records that a watchlist-style match
occurred, without prematurely asserting a Block decision.

## Important limitation — a match is not a decision

A `compliance_hold` flag records only that a name-matching event occurred. It is **never**
sufficient on its own to Clear, Block, or Escalate a payment. That determination is made
separately by the Compliance Agent, which must retrieve the specific matched entry, weigh it
against jurisdiction and corridor rules, and cite the exact document it relied on before
reaching Clear / Block / Escalate. Any decision whose computed risk exceeds this
institution's configured threshold is escalated by policy regardless of what the match itself
suggests.
