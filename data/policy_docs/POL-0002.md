# Payment Processing SLA & Timeout Escalation Policy

## Rule

Once a cross-border credit transfer is submitted to a correspondent or clearing rail, this
institution expects a rail acknowledgement (settlement confirmation or explicit rejection)
within 24 hours under standard conditions, and no later than 48 hours under degraded
conditions (rail-side outage, holiday calendar misalignment, intermediary bank delay).

## Escalation threshold

If no rail acknowledgement has been received after **48 hours** from submission, the payment
is moved from `flagged` to `stuck` status with cause `timeout`, and the processing log records
the elapsed-time detail (e.g. "no rail acknowledgement received after 48h"). A `stuck` payment
is not resubmitted automatically — resubmission risks creating a duplicate (see `POL-0003`).

## Rationale

A payment with no acknowledgement after 48 hours is more likely to indicate a rail-side
failure, a routing error, or a hold on the receiving side than a transient delay. Treating it
as `stuck` rather than silently waiting indefinitely ensures a human reviews the payment's
actual status with the correspondent bank before any further action is taken.
