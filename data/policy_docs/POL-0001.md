# Remittance Information Requirement Policy

## Rule

Every outbound cross-border credit transfer initiated through this institution must carry a
populated `RmtInf` (Remittance Information) field in its ISO 20022 `pain.001` message. A
transfer submitted without `RmtInf` does not fail XML validation — the field is optional and
schema-valid when absent — but it fails this institution's own payment policy.

## Rationale

Remittance information is the primary reference a receiving institution and the beneficiary
use to reconcile an incoming payment against an invoice, order, or contract. Without it,
downstream reconciliation and AML reference-matching cannot be performed reliably, and the
payment cannot be released.

## Enforcement

A payment ingested with no `RmtInf/Ustrd` value present anywhere in its `CdtTrfTxInf` block is
flagged with cause `missing_malformed_field` and held in `flagged` status pending manual
review. The payment is not returned to the sender automatically — a reviewer must either
obtain the missing remittance detail out-of-band or confirm the transfer is a legitimate
exception before release.
