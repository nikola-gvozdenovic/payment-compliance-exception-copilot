# Duplicate Payment Detection Policy

## Rule

A payment is treated as a **duplicate** when it matches another payment processed within the
preceding 15 minutes on all three of the following: `EndToEndId`, transfer amount, and
creditor. A match on amount and creditor alone, without a matching `EndToEndId`, is not
sufficient on its own to flag a duplicate — coincidental repeat payments to the same creditor
for the same amount do occur legitimately (e.g. recurring invoices) and must not be
auto-flagged on amount/creditor alone.

## Enforcement

When all three fields match a payment processed within the window (for example, two minutes
apart), the later payment is flagged with cause `duplicate` and held in `flagged` status. The
processing log records which earlier payment it matches and the elapsed time between the two
submissions.

## Rationale

Duplicate submission most commonly results from a sender-side retry after an unacknowledged
first attempt (see `POL-0002`), rather than fraud — but releasing both instances would result
in the beneficiary being paid twice. Holding the later instance for confirmation prevents this
without assuming malicious intent.
