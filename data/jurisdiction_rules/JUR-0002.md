# USD → CHF Corridor Rule

## Rule

Any credit transfer moving from a USD-denominated debtor account to a CHF-denominated
creditor account, for an amount at or above 25,000.00 USD, requires an additional
beneficiary-bank BIC verification step before release, given the more limited number of
direct USD/CHF correspondent relationships compared to major EUR/USD corridors.

## Enforcement

A USD→CHF payment meeting the threshold above receives an additional automated BIC-format
verification step during processing. This is a routing-quality check, not itself a compliance
hold — a failed BIC check should be handled as a `missing_malformed_field` case per `POL-0001`
rather than a corridor-specific flag.

## Rationale

USD→CHF transfers more frequently route through an intermediary correspondent than direct
same-region corridors, increasing the chance of a stale or misformatted beneficiary BIC.
Catching this before submission reduces downstream `timeout` cases (see `POL-0002`).
