# EUR → EUR (Domestic / Eurozone-Internal) Rule

## Rule

A credit transfer where both the debtor and creditor accounts are EUR-denominated (a
same-currency, eurozone-internal transfer) is subject to this institution's baseline review
threshold rather than a corridor-specific enhanced-diligence threshold: standard AML
screening (`POL-0004`) and standard field-completeness checks (`POL-0001`) apply, with no
additional corridor-specific verification step.

## Rationale

Same-currency eurozone-internal transfers do not cross a currency boundary and typically
settle over a single, well-established clearing mechanism (e.g. SEPA-equivalent), so the
elevated beneficiary-bank-verification risk seen on cross-currency corridors (see `JUR-0001`,
`JUR-0002`) does not apply here. Baseline screening remains mandatory — this rule lowers the
corridor-specific burden, not the underlying compliance requirements.
