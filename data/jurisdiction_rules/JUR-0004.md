# USD → USD (Domestic) Rule

## Rule

A credit transfer where both the debtor and creditor accounts are USD-denominated (a
same-currency, US-domestic transfer) follows this institution's baseline review threshold:
standard AML screening (`POL-0004`) and standard field-completeness checks (`POL-0001`)
apply. Same-day settlement rails (e.g. same-day ACH) are permitted for transfers below
10,000.00 USD; transfers at or above that amount are routed over wire rather than same-day
ACH, per this institution's internal rail-selection policy.

## Rationale

US-domestic transfers settle over well-established, same-jurisdiction rails, so no
corridor-specific enhanced-diligence step (unlike `JUR-0001`, `JUR-0002`) is warranted.
Rail selection by amount exists to balance settlement speed against per-item cost, not as a
compliance control.
