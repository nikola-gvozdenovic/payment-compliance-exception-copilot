# EUR → GBP Corridor Rule

## Rule

Any credit transfer moving from a EUR-denominated debtor account to a GBP-denominated
creditor account, for an amount at or above 5,000.00 in the transfer's settlement currency,
requires enhanced due diligence before release: confirmation of the creditor's UK beneficiary
bank details and a manual corridor-risk review.

## Enforcement

A EUR→GBP payment meeting the threshold above is flagged with cause
`corridor_currency_issue` and held in `flagged` status for manual policy review. The
processing log records the corridor pairing explicitly (e.g. "EUR-GBP corridor flagged for
manual policy review").

## Rationale

The EUR→GBP corridor carries a higher historical rate of beneficiary-bank detail mismatches
following post-Brexit clearing changes, making enhanced verification proportionate for
higher-value transfers on this specific corridor.
